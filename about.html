Est. 2024 · Independent tool

A QR generator that keeps your data where it belongs — with you.

QuickMark started as a simple question: why does generating a QR code require sending your link, WiFi password, or contact details to someone else's server? It doesn't. Here's how we built one that never has to.

Local by design

Every QR code is drawn pixel-by-pixel in your browser's own JavaScript engine. There's no API call, no upload, and no server that ever sees what you typed.

No accounts, ever

We don't ask for an email address to unlock features, and we never will. Open the page, generate your code, move on with your day.

Built to last

No frameworks, no build pipeline, no dependency chain that can break. Just HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — the same stack the web was built on.

Why we built it this way

Most "free" QR generators aren't really free — they trade convenience for your data, adding tracking pixels, mandatory accounts, or watermarks that only disappear behind a paywall. We wanted something closer to a utility: a tool you reach for the way you'd reach for a calculator, without wondering what happens to the numbers afterward.

That constraint shaped every decision. Generating QR codes entirely client-side meant we couldn't rely on a backend to handle complex encoding logic, so the encoder itself runs directly in your browser tab. It meant giving up "cloud sync" for your QR history, because keeping no server means keeping no history at all. We think that trade-off is the right one.

Who this is for

QuickMark is built for anyone who needs a QR code and would rather not think twice about it: small business owners printing a menu, event organizers sharing a WiFi password, developers linking to a repo, or anyone pasting a link into a design file. If you can type it, you can encode it.

What's next

We keep the tool intentionally small, but we're always refining the details — better color contrast checking, more content types, and continued attention to accessibility and performance. If there's something you'd like to see, the contact page reaches us directly.
