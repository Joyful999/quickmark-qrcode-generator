Privacy Policy

The short version: QuickMark has no backend, so there is nothing to send your data to. The long version is below.

1. What we collect

QuickMark does not collect, store, or transmit the content you enter into the QR code generator — not the URLs, text, phone numbers, WiFi credentials, or any other field. All encoding happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing you type is ever sent to a server, because this website does not operate one for that purpose.

2. Contact form data

If you use the contact form, your message is opened in your own email application via a "mailto" link. We do not receive or process that message through this website directly; it is sent only if and when you choose to send the resulting email from your own account.

3. Hosting logs

Like virtually any website, our hosting provider may keep basic server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps) for security and reliability purposes. These logs are not linked to anything you generate with the QR tool itself.

4. Cookies and local storage

QuickMark uses your browser's local storage only to remember a single preference: whether you last chose light or dark mode. This value stays on your device and is never transmitted anywhere. We do not use tracking cookies, analytics cookies, or third-party advertising identifiers.

5. Third-party services

This site loads web fonts from Google Fonts to render its typography. Loading a font may share standard request metadata (like your IP address) with that provider, consistent with how any embedded web font works. We do not embed advertising networks, analytics trackers, or session-recording scripts.

6. Children's privacy

QuickMark is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information from any user at all.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top of this page will change with it. We encourage you to check back occasionally if you rely on this policy for compliance purposes.

8. Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent through our contact page.
